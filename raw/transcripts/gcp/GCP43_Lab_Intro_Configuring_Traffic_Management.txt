In this lab, you will create a regional internal application load balancer with
two back ends. Each back end will be
an instance group. You will configure
the load balancer to create a blue
green deployment; the blue deployment refers to the current version
of your application. The green deployment refers
to a new application version. In this lab exercise, you configure the
load balancer to send 70% of the traffic to
the blue deployment, and 30% to the green deployment.
