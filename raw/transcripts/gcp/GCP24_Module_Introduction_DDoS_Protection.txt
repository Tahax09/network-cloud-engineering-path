Welcome to the distributed
denial-of-service attacks, DDoS, protection module. Distributed denial-of-service
attacks are a major concern today. They can have a huge and potentially fatal
impact on businesses, if the business is not
adequately prepared. We will start this module with a quick discussion of
how DDoS attacks work, and then we will review some DDoS mitigation techniques that are provided
by Google Cloud. Then we will finish
up with a review of complimentary partner products
and a lab where you will get a chance to see
