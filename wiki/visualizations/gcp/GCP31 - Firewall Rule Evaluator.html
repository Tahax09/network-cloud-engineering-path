What happens on the wire

The firewall is enforced at each VM's virtual NIC, not at a central box. Dropped packets disappear silently: no RST and no ICMP unreachable comes back. A tracked connection stays open while at least one packet passes every 10 minutes.

Kwan's problem

Cymbal needs firewalling at several levels: org-wide rules from security, per-department exceptions, then the app teams' own rules. Cloud NGFW evaluates each level in order. Set what each level does with this packet:
