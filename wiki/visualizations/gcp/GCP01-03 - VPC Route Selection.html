    Build a subnet

    Course vs current docs: the lecture says IPv6-only subnets don't exist. Google Cloud docs now list three stack types: IPv4-only, dual-stack and IPv6-only. An IPv6-only VM reaches IPv4 destinations through DNS64 and NAT64. You can't convert a subnet between IPv6-only and the other two types.

    Advertise your prefixes

    Your company owns 203.0.112.0/20. You're moving part of it to Google Cloud with BYOIP. Internet routers pick the longest (most specific) prefix that contains the destination.

    Where the internet sends it

    Smallest BYOIP prefix Google accepts: /24 for IPv4 (/48 for IPv6). Longer prefixes are filtered by most internet routers. Before Google can advertise your prefix, you have to prove you own it with an RPKI ROA.
